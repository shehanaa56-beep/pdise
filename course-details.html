FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Find answers to common questions about the PDISE course, admission, fees, and training schedule.

Ask Our Counselors →
What is the course duration?
The PDISE program runs for 12 months, providing a comprehensive 800 hours of intensive classroom training, laboratory practicals, and a guided capstone internship.
Who can join this course?
Any graduate (B.Sc, B.Com, B.Tech, BCA, BA, BBA), final-year college student, or working professional with basic computer familiarity can join. Prior programming experience is not required as we start from foundational basics in software engineering.
Is it classroom training?
Yes! This is 100% offline classroom training conducted in well-equipped computer labs at our primary centers in Perinthalmanna and Manjeri, Malappuram DT, with direct mentor guidance.
Will I get an internship?
Yes! Every enrolled student completes a hands-on live internship project on actual software development tasks, culminating in an ISO & MSME recognized Internship Certificate.
What are the fee payment options and EMI plans?
The total course fee is ₹70,000. You can register with ₹10,000 and pay the remaining balance in 12 comfortable monthly EMI installments of ₹5,000. Students paying in full upfront are eligible for a ₹2,000 cash back limited offer.
What career placement support does SINET provide?
Our career assistance wing provides resume enhancement, portfolio curation on GitHub, LinkedIn optimization, mock interviews, and active interview referral scheduling with hiring companies across Kerala and beyond.
